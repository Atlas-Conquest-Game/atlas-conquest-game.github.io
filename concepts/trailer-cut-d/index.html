What is Atlas Conquest?

Build a deck of cards that become units on a real battlefield. Play as a commander leading an army for your patron god, battle real players online, outmaneuver your rival and conquer the map.

  • Minion Cards are played on the physical battlefield.

  • Spell Cards impact the battlefield!

  • Claim tiles to grow your mana each turn.

  • Free to play
  • 1 vs 1 online
  • 16 commanders
  • 280+ cards

Every card will be unlockable, free to play.

Three Battlefields

  • Dunes

    Desert ruins

  • Snowmelt

    Frozen glacier

  • Tropics

    Jungle island

Choose Your Commander

Every commander serves a patron god, and each god plays its own way. Pick a god to see its style, or a commander to see what they do.

Sixteen commanders Your commander’s patron god decides which cards you can play.
